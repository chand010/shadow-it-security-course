# Course Roadmap

## Delivery model
Progress from foundations to a simulated organizational assessment. Each module includes explanation, hands-on work, evidence capture, reflection and a knowledge check. Use synthetic information only.

## Module sequence

### 01 — Shadow IT fundamentals
Define Shadow IT, unsanctioned SaaS, shadow data and shadow AI. Explore why staff adopt services outside formal procurement. Deliverable: fictional application inventory and initial risk hypotheses.

### 02 — Discovery and visibility
Establish authorization, scope, privacy and proportionality. Study procurement records, identity logs, DNS/proxy telemetry, endpoint inventory, expense records and staff interviews. Tools: Wireshark, Zeek, Wazuh and cloud identity audit logs. Deliverable: documented discovery plan and evidence register. Never monitor networks without permission.

### 03 — Cloud storage security
Study SaaS sharing, public links, external collaborators, sync clients and retention. Lab: isolated Nextcloud tenant with fictional users and documents. Review sharing permissions, MFA, audit events and deletion/retention. Deliverable: cloud-storage configuration review.

### 04 — Identity, IAM and PAM
Study authentication vs authorization, SSO, MFA, federation, lifecycle, least privilege, role design, joiner/mover/leaver processes and privileged access. Optional lab: Keycloak. Deliverable: role matrix and privileged-access review.

### 05 — CASB, SSE, SASE and SSPM
Understand visibility, policy enforcement, inline/API controls, posture management and secure access. Illustrative platforms: Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps, Netskope, Zscaler and Obsidian Security. Deliverable: use-case-to-control map.

### 06 — SaaS management
Study application inventory, ownership, license optimization, renewals, shadow account discovery and offboarding. Examples: ServiceNow, Zylo, Torii, BetterCloud, Flexera, Zluri and CloudEagle. Deliverable: SaaS register and ownership workflow.

### 07 — Gartner research and vendor evaluation
Learn Magic Quadrant, Critical Capabilities, Hype Cycle, Market Guides and Strategic Technology Trends. Evaluate report scope, methodology, date and limitations. Validate vendor claims with primary documentation and proof of concept. Deliverable: transparent platform evaluation matrix.

### 08 — DLP and data governance
Study data classification, ownership, residency, retention, legal holds, data minimization, DLP policy lifecycle, coverage, false positives and exceptions. Microsoft Purview is one example. Deliverable: synthetic data classification and DLP proposal.

### 09 — Monitoring and incident response
Study logging, alert triage, investigation, containment, recovery and lessons learned. Lab options: Wazuh, Zeek and synthetic cloud audit events. Deliverable: incident timeline and response playbook.

### 10 — Governance and court assessment
Define scope, stakeholders, evidence handling, risk scoring and recommendations. Build a prioritized roadmap with owners, milestones and measurable outcomes. Deliverable: executive report and presentation based on a fictional court scenario.

## Completion criteria
A module is complete only when the learner can explain the concepts, has executed the practical exercise or documented a blocker, has sanitized evidence, answered the knowledge check and saved the deliverable.

## Suggested rhythm
A daily session can include 20–30 minutes of concepts, 30–60 minutes of lab work and 10 minutes of notes. Adjust to hardware and available time; avoid running multiple memory-heavy platforms simultaneously.
