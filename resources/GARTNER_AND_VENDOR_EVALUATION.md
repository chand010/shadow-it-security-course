# Gartner Research and Enterprise Platform Evaluation

## Gartner's role
Gartner is an IT research and advisory organization. It is not a Shadow IT security product or an installable lab control. Its research can help teams understand markets, compare vendor positioning and identify evaluation questions.

Research is one input, not a substitute for security requirements, technical validation, procurement review or legal assessment.

## Research formats
- **Magic Quadrant:** Visual market analysis positioning vendors according to stated methodology and criteria. Read the scope and definitions; placement is not a universal verdict.
- **Critical Capabilities:** Comparative analysis of product capabilities against defined use cases and criteria.
- **Hype Cycle:** Framework describing maturity and expectations around technologies; not a product security rating.
- **Market Guide:** Describes a market, use cases, representative providers and trends.
- **Strategic Technology Trends:** Broader trends that may influence enterprise planning.

Access may require a Gartner subscription. Do not copy or redistribute copyrighted report content. Record report title, publication date, scope and your own concise analysis.

## Categories and illustrative platforms

| Category | Typical focus | Illustrative examples |
|---|---|---|
| CASB | Visibility and policy controls for cloud service use | Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps; Netskope |
| SSE | Cloud-delivered security such as secure web gateway, CASB and ZTNA | Netskope; Zscaler |
| SASE | Converged networking and security delivered as a service | Zscaler and other providers |
| SSPM | SaaS application security posture and configuration risk | Obsidian Security and other providers |
| SaaS management | SaaS inventory, ownership, licenses, spend and lifecycle | ServiceNow, Zylo, Torii, BetterCloud, Flexera, Zluri, CloudEagle |
| DLP | Preventing inappropriate disclosure or movement of sensitive data | Microsoft Purview and other DLP providers |

Categories overlap and product capabilities change. Verify current features, licensing, regional availability and integrations with vendors. Examples are not endorsements or claims about current Gartner positioning.

## Practical vendor evaluation
Fictional court requirement: evaluate platforms that may help discover unmanaged cloud use and govern sensitive information.

Suggested weighted criteria:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Shadow IT discovery | 20% |
| Cloud security controls | 20% |
| DLP and data governance | 15% |
| IAM and privileged access integration | 15% |
| Monitoring and reporting | 15% |
| Cost, deployment and operational effort | 15% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Score each product against documented requirements on a consistent scale, such as 1 (does not meet) to 5 (fully meets). Define each score before evaluation. Mark unknowns as unknown; missing evidence is not a positive score.

For each criterion record the requirement, vendor evidence and URL, evidence type (claim, documentation, demonstration or tested result), licensing assumptions, dependencies, data residency and privacy questions, integration effort, operational ownership, gaps and follow-up questions.

## Exercise
Select three candidate platforms across relevant categories. Build a comparison using the criteria above. Use current vendor documentation and legitimately available Gartner research. Do not invent Gartner ratings or vendor capabilities.

## Evaluation principle
Start with organizational use cases and requirements, then assess products. Do not assume a vendor's position in a market report automatically makes it the right choice.
